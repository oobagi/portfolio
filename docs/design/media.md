# Image & Media Strategy

> Part of the [alexcarpenter.me design reference](overview.md)

## Image Philosophy

Images are **proof of work**, not decoration. The only images on the site are project screenshots and company logos. No headshot, no illustrations, no icons (except inline SVGs for arrows and external link indicators), no background images.

## Image Style

- **Project screenshots:** Full-width within the 468px column. Likely bordered or contained. Show the actual product.
- **Company logos:** Small, inline with experience entry text. Functional, not decorative.
- **External link icons:** Inline SVG arrows indicating outbound links.

## Image Placement

All images are contained within the content column. No full-bleed, no overlapping, no floating, no background usage. At 468px, images are always a known fixed width — no responsive image complexity.

## Media Density

Very low. The homepage has ~3 project images and ~6 small company logos across the entire scroll. The vast majority of the page is text. The notes page has no images at all.

## Absence of Images

In sections without images, the visual space is filled by:
- **Whitespace** — the primary design material
- **Typography** — monospace text at various densities
- **Structural elements** — `<mark>` highlights, `<details>` toggles, `<dl>` lists
- **The blue CTA buttons** — the only high-contrast elements

The site proves that a compelling portfolio can be built almost entirely from text.
