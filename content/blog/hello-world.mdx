**HELLO!** This is my first ever blog! How awkward.

How does this work? I'm just supposed to start talking?

I guess that would make sense. *But how?*

Our brain knows *exactly* how to talk to themselves. They almost speak their own language. I can feel my brain almost having a conversation with itself in the back of my head at all times. I can't always tell you what it's talking about, but I know it ain't quiet!



With this said, trying to wrangle my thoughts into one coherent story is one of the hardest things I've done! I built this blog with the hopes that I can force myself to get better at it. I've always been a decent listener *(my girlfriend might disagree)*, but talking has never been my strength *(my girlfriend would very much agree)*. 

As ironic as it is, being a programmer, typing 100x more than the average person, you would think English, my first programming language (AI joke HA), would come naturally.

This is why I'm a bad vibe-coder.
